Privacy Policy

Auto Shorts Factory · Effective date: 15 November 2026

1. Who this applies to

Auto Shorts Factory is a personal automation tool used only by its developer on the developer's own social media accounts. It has no other users and does not collect personal data from the public.

2. Data the tool handles

  • Authorization tokens for the developer's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts, used solely to publish videos. They are stored as encrypted secrets and are never shared.
  • Public trend data (for example publicly listed trending topics), used to choose video subjects. This contains no personal data.

The tool does not read, store or analyse other users' data, messages, followers or viewing activity.

3. Sharing and sale

No data is sold or shared with third parties, other than sending the developer's own videos and captions to the platforms they are published on, and using cloud services (such as Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud) to generate content.

4. Retention and deletion

Authorization tokens are kept only while the tool is in use and can be revoked at any time from each platform's account settings, which immediately ends the tool's access.

5. Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/SisioGio/auto-shorts-factory-legal/issues.
